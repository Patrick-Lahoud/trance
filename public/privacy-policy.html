Trance Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Trance is a desktop focus and attention trainer. This policy explains what Trance processes and how that information is handled.

Information Trance processes

While a session is active, Trance may process cursor movement, keyboard activity, idle time, foreground application information, task names, break periods, and focus estimates. Trance may identify common video players or video services so passive video watching is not incorrectly treated as distraction.

Where data is stored

Session history, task names, and settings are stored locally on your device. Trance does not require an account and does not intentionally upload this information to a remote server.

What Trance does not collect

Trance does not record the contents of your keystrokes, screenshots, webcam video, microphone audio, or the contents of documents and websites.

Sharing and third parties

Trance does not sell or share session data for advertising. The application may use standard operating-system APIs and bundled open-source libraries to provide its desktop functionality.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it using the application’s available session controls or remove Trance’s local application data. Uninstalling the application may not remove separately retained user data unless you choose to delete it.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Trance changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Trance publisher through the channel where you obtained the application.
